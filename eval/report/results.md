## How the labels were made

Pairs were pooled from the top 10 of `dense` and `hybrid+ce` and pre-labelled by a
model (`docs/05-evaluation.md`).
Of 1142 gold rows, 680 were checked by a human with the pre-label shown and 462 were labelled by a second model (Claude), blind to the pre-labels, and not checked by a human. Decided by Person A for lack of time; see `data/gold/provenance.json`.
The human checkers changed 11 of 680 pre-labels (1.6%).
The second model disagreed with 25 of 462 pre-labels (5.4%), mostly by rejecting partial matches the pre-labels had accepted.
Unchecked rows are excluded from every number above.

Unlabelled pairs count as 0. That slightly favours the strategies that fed the
pool, which is a known property of pooled collections rather than a fault here.
